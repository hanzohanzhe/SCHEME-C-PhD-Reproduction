# Third-party material

The source repository does not contain the approximately 842 MB UK benchmark
payload. That payload is distributed as a separate private release and retains
the per-object terms described in:

- `benchmark-data-metadata/SOURCE_REGISTER.md`;
- `benchmark-data-metadata/LICENSING.md`;
- `benchmark-data-metadata/bill-of-data.json`.

The Apache-2.0 software licence does not relicense those datasets. Historical
logs and result tables are owner-authored research artefacts; references to
local paths in those immutable logs are provenance evidence, not installation
instructions.
