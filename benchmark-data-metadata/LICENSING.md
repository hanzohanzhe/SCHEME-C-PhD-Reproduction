# Licensing and redistribution boundary

This repository and its private Release aggregate objects from mixed upstream
providers. Local possession, private research use and inclusion here do not
replace the licence attached to each source object.

- DESNZ REPD derivatives retain Open Government Licence v3.0 attribution.
- NESO objects retain the licence stated for the exact source dataset.
- ERA5-derived objects retain Copernicus/ECMWF attribution and adaptation
  information.
- Ember price inputs retain CC BY 4.0 attribution.
- Hanzhe Xing's FORCE configuration and aggregation material retains its stated
  repository/document licence.

The repository is private while per-object public redistribution and personal-
data review remain open. `SOURCE_REGISTER.md` is the controlling human-readable
source register; `local-source-inventory.json` and `bill-of-data.json` are the
machine-readable evidence.
